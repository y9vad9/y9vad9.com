My username, y9vad9, often sparks curiosity, so let me break it down for you. It's pronounced as "Я Вадя" (Ya Vadya), which is a playful nod to my name, Vadym, and has roots in [LEET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leet) speak.

The inspiration came from one of my friends – y9san9, when I wanted to create a unique username not used across all social platforms.

## Details

The y and 9 combine to sound like "Я" (ya) in Russian/Ukrainian, which means "I" or "Me."
The vad comes directly from my name, Vadym.
The 9 at the end is a stylized way to close it all off, mirroring the "9" in the beginning for symmetry.
So, y9vad9 translates to "I am Vadya" (a casual diminutive form of Vadym). The resulting way of pronouncing it is 'ya-vad-ya'.
