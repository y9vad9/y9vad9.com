RSS (Really Simple Syndication) allows you to stay updated with your favorite websites without having to check them manually. By using an RSS reader, you can consolidate all your news and blog updates in one place.

## Available Feeds

I provide separate feeds for each language. You can copy the links below into your RSS reader:

*   **English**: [/en/feed.xml](/en/feed.xml)
*   **Deutsch**: [/de/feed.xml](/de/feed.xml)
*   **Українська**: [/uk/feed.xml](/uk/feed.xml)

## How to use?

Most modern RSS readers (like Feedly, Inoreader, or NetNewsWire) will automatically detect these links if you paste them into the "Add Content" or "Subscribe" field.
